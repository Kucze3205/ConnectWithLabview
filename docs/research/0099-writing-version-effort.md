The JSON gives eight cost drivers with units, shared/version-dependent work, dependencies and uncertainty. Reuse reading and dependency models once; charge encoding, creation defaults, preservation, packaging/build adapters and writing-validation increments separately. Extend existing benchmark infrastructure rather than re-adding it. Historical wrapper figures concern reading interfaces; MCP changes access, not compatibility. New day ranges remain unknown: no timed semantic write/rebuild sample or count of incompatible encoding/schema/target strata supports them. Machine job-hours and unavailable inputs are separate from engineer-days.

Track **original save version, emitted editing format and validation runtime separately**. Preserve/hash originals; independently freeze semantic changes, allowed file/object differences and per-file version evidence; check every emitted/propagated source against its original, or a creation's requested target. Unknown/conflicting version fields remain unverified. A separate provisioned native job must distinguish load, build, controlled behavior and target execution on frozen disposable outputs, recording conversions. Newer-runtime loading alone cannot prove same-save-version preservation.

A strict 2024-format fallback loses older/later originals; historically, 413/415 versioned RSRC inputs were outside 2024, an illustration rather than a scope or prevalence estimate. Keeping an old original alongside a converted copy prevents destruction but does not preserve the edited output's old compatibility. Supporting old-format outputs restores the corresponding encoding work. Source versions, package/build tools, modules and runtime availability remain unresolved; the recorded 2026 headless compilation neither provisions 2024 nor supplies an authoring capability. Offline editing feasibility and native compilation/execution are distinct. **No fallback is selected or presently evidence-supported; silence grants no acceptance.**

## What next

The decisive uncertainty is semantic encoding plus integrity/cache preservation and its reuse across formats. A controlled, independently authored same-version one-change pair could expose dependent changes; an eventual edited artifact also needs original-version native load/behavior checks. Neither that pair nor native capability is staged, so no decisive experiment is currently ready.

The **one smallest ready follow-up** is a version-evidence audit of WM1's exact [pinned original](../../benchmark/writing/method.json), whose prepared-file SHA256 was checked here: `19b5dcd98b3b2e83e2b533d9227bd07d16837f350243377292b7c0b8a935338c`. With pinned pylavi and Python, record every `vers`/`LVSR` entry's raw version bytes and decoded values, including disagreement or missing authority. This narrows WM1's declared unknown version, not writer feasibility. Stop after one original record, or immediately on a pin mismatch/parse failure; do not substitute fixtures, edit files or scan the corpus. Exact inputs, output and resume limits are in JSON `next_measurement`; retain saved POC evidence and closed reviews.
