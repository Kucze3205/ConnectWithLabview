# Pin-drift classification of the 10 writing checkers (pin-drift-0218)

Measurement only. No checker, pin map, verification.json or input was edited and nothing was re-pinned. No LabVIEW behaviour is claimed; benchmark_ready stays false. Checkers were re-run as `python3 -I -B <file>` from the repository root; full outputs are in `.session/artifacts/<checker>.py.out` (stdout, then `--STDERR--`, then stderr).

Terms. `pin-drift-only`: the complete output was read, every failing check is a pin or hash comparison, and all content checks that ran passed. `content-drift`: a checked content field changed. `unknown`: the checker stops at its first failing pin, so later checks never ran. For these, "pin drift observed" is the only established fact.

The drifted files are the two pin indexes `benchmark/preparation.json` and `benchmark/writing/input-pins.json`, plus two pin manifests for the C1 package (`c1-challenges-0125-pins.json`, `reviews-0128/c1-artifact-manifest-0126.json`; the latter sits in `reviews-0128/`, not at `benchmark/writing/`). What changed in them is re-pinned hashes of challenge fixtures and `score.py` (see Drift table).

## Recommendation

1. No re-pin was done and none is decided here. Whether to refresh frozen pins is a Policymaker or owner decision. README.md:60 says the #544 checker intentionally stops matching current inputs; treat the frozen snapshots as frozen.
2. The 0115, 0117, 0119 and 0121 packages fail only on `preparation.json` and `input-pins.json` pin drift. After the #758 to #905 re-pins of challenge fixtures, their text key, original.lvproj hashes and counterexamples are unaffected. The WP1 text key is therefore not shown wrong by these failures. Neither is it shown right by a pass, which cannot occur on the current tree.
3. The next shaper should pick one route: (a) a new checker that pins the current `preparation.json` and `input-pins.json` and leaves the frozen ones untouched; or (b) a read-only replay that checks the old pins against `git show fc02ca67:benchmark/preparation.json` and `git show 2033c33f:benchmark/writing/input-pins.json`. Route (b) would run the 6 `unknown` checkers on the historical bytes and show whether anything beyond pins fails, without re-pinning.
4. A cheap discriminator for the 6 `unknown` checkers: replay each in a scratch copy of the tree at the commits listed in the Drift table. That is a separate task, not run here.
