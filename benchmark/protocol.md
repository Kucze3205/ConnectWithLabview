# Benchmark method draft

Unpinned, unreviewed design for issue #141; no tasks, keys, split, runs or challenge results are established by this document. No benchmark readiness or product quality is claimed.
Authority: [evaluation contract](../docs/perpetua/evaluation.md), [goal](../goal/goal.md), [accepted limits](../docs/perpetua/accepted-limits.md). Current evidence: [sources](sources.md) and [coverage](coverage.md); native semantic inspection of that inventory has not run.
Public sources are development material, including their copies and variants. Missing families and versions remain gaps in the full goal; the 2024 fallback is not selected.

## tasks

Method: create a case specification before testing any reader: question, original artifact identity, family/save version, required dependencies/environment, expected observable, oracle procedure, source locator and explicit uncertainty. Start with public development cases; create withheld cases only inside an approved restricted boundary.
Each case asks for concrete claims whose correctness and source grounding can be checked individually. A statement about execution must identify its inputs, state, target and conditions; structural evidence alone cannot establish hardware behavior.

| Family / task | Question and observable to establish independently | Original source to cite |
|---|---|---|
| VI and VIM | Explain computation, types, structures, dataflow and execution dependencies for controlled inputs; distinguish wired order from scheduling assumptions and specialization. | Panel terminals, diagram nodes/wires, structure/case paths and connector pane; VIM call-site specialization. |
| CTL, typedefs and custom controls | Identify type/defaults, strictness and consumers; predict which consumers change or break after a controlled typedef edit. | Control/type properties and original consumer terminals; before/after native observations on derived copies. |
| Classes and libraries | Explain membership, scope, inheritance, dispatch and dependency resolution. | LVCLASS/LVLIB item/property paths plus relevant method VI diagrams and native properties. |
| Projects and build specifications | Find declared and resolved dependencies, missing items, target settings and build inclusion/exclusion; separate declarations from successful resolution/build. | LVPROJ XML item/property paths and native project/build property views; dependent original files. |
| Diagram changes | Explain semantic effects of a controlled pair: behavior-changing edits, presentation-only edits and uncertain effects. | Both original revision hashes and corresponding nodes/wires/cases; native comparison and controlled I/O evidence. |
| Menus and resources | Explain menu hierarchy, tags, actions and resource references, including missing resources. | MNU item hierarchy/native properties and referring VI event/handling locations; original resource entries. |
| Aliases, project state and configuration | Identify alias mappings and relevant configuration/analysis settings; establish whether a setting is runtime, editor or machine-specific before claiming an effect. | ALIASES, LVLPS and VIANCFG original keys/XML paths plus native interpretation where necessary. |
| Packages and archives | Explain dependency constraints, installation contents and contained artifact relationships; identify unresolved or nested members. | VIP/VIPB/VIPC/VSIX/ZIP original manifest/member paths and member hashes; native/package-manager evidence for installation claims. |
| Packed libraries and compiled distributions | Identify exposed APIs, linkage and execution requirements; state when diagrams are absent and semantic reading is unavailable. | LVLIBP/native exports and original distribution metadata; controlled API calls where executable. |
| Missing and future families | Design dedicated cases for templates, globals, LLBs, shared libraries, tests, XControls/XNodes, executables, real-time/FPGA targets and hardware/module resources; classify CTT/GBL/GLB/LSB/LVSC/LVTEST/RTEXE/RTM/UIR/VIT/XCTL/XNODE by native evidence. | Original family-specific objects/properties, test definitions, target/build settings and observable behavior; unknown formats remain unresolved. |

Archive members inherit their semantic family tasks as well as container/linkage tasks; archive counts do not demonstrate readability. Extension/magic inventory is not an exhaustive family definition.
Gradeable answer record: `case_id`, `status`, `claims[{claim_id, answer, conditions, citations[]}]`, `limitations`. Citations contain original SHA256, repository-relative path, archive/member chain when applicable, and a native object/property locator or exact XML/key/byte location appropriate to the claim.
For diagrams, record owning VI, nested structure/case, object/terminal/wire identity and native view region; attach a hashed native capture and object map when identifiers are unstable. A screenshot or exported node ID alone is insufficient: the reviewer must resolve the claim back into the preserved original.
Before reader exposure, freeze an independently checked reference inventory per task: stable required-claim IDs, atomic facts/relations, conditions, acceptable alternatives, numerical tolerances, original/native reference locators and hashes, and disallowed inferences. Identify separately the required answer claims and required reader observations; neither inventory may be inferred from submitted answers or exports. Record inventory/key revisions and unresolved references. Development keys may be public; withheld keys, case details and revealing evidence must never enter this repository or its history.
Account for every required ID and every submitted factual assertion, including implicit assertions in prose and limitations. Preserve the submitted text and its mapping to reference IDs; split compound assertions into atomic claims under frozen rules. Repeated equivalent assertions earn credit once; omissions retain their required slots. An assertion and its negation are a contradiction even when one matches the key: mark the affected item incorrect and retain both assertions. Unmatched assertions enter a separate extra-claim ledger and require independent checking; true extras cannot replace omissions, and false or unverified extras cannot disappear from scoring. Freeze these mapping/adjudication rules before runs and retain scorer/checker decisions.
Score reading fidelity, answer correctness and source grounding separately using the following item rules; faithful extraction alone does not establish correct reasoning, and answer correctness cannot establish fidelity.

| Dimension | Required unit and correct outcome | Other item outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| Reading fidelity | Each fixed required reader observation matches its independently checked native reference, including required types, properties and relations under declared tolerances. | Partially represented compound content is partial; a conflicting value/relation is incorrect; absent content is missing. Unsupported access, inaccessible diagrams, tool errors and unverified native references retain their explicit outcomes and never earn correct credit. |
| Answer correctness | Each required answer claim matches an independently established expected claim under its conditions and tolerances. | Incomplete content is partial; false claims or contradictions are incorrect; omitted claims are missing; claims without sufficient independent evidence are unverified. An unsupported-case declaration remains unsupported, not correct. |
| Source grounding | Each required answer claim has resolvable original-artifact citations supporting its complete asserted content and conditions. | Support for only part is partial; a wrong or non-supporting citation is incorrect; absent claims/citations are missing; inaccessible evidence is unverified. A correct answer with a wrong citation is incorrect in grounding independently of correctness. |

Reader-observation contract: before exposure, an independent preparer freezes probe IDs, artifact/member hashes, reader entry points and arguments, observation scope, required observation IDs and native-reference alignment rules. Choose probes from the task/native inventories, independently of agent answers and reader outputs; include the types, objects, wires, properties and relationships needed by the task. Pin ordering, limits, any adaptive branches and their selection rules; record unprobed scope as missing coverage. No favorable agent-selected calls may substitute for required probes.
For every probe, preserve the exact request, reader/configuration revision, environment, raw response bytes or files and hashes, timestamps, exit/error records and any truncation. Preserve empty responses and failed calls. A separate alignment record maps raw-output locations to required native-reference IDs and records absent, extra, duplicate and contradictory assertions; normalization must retain raw evidence and cannot invent missing facts. Unsupported operations, missing dependencies/licenses/environment, protected/removed diagrams, empty or truncated required content, errors and unverified references never count as matching observations. A successful call or valid wrapper alone earns no fidelity credit.
Freeze denominators per task and dimension: all required answer IDs for correctness and grounding, and all required observation IDs for fidelity. Report counts in every item-outcome category over those fixed totals, plus separate extra-claim counts by outcome and contradiction/duplicate counts; do not enlarge denominators with submitted claims or remove unavailable items. Report all planned cases as the coverage denominator alongside attempted/executed/scored counts. Any conditional summary must also show its eligible denominator and excluded/unavailable counts; no single aggregate merges the three dimensions.
Apply the following case-assessment rules in order, separately for each dimension. An absent or empty required reference inventory is unverified, never vacuously correct. With an established inventory, any incorrect required item or extra assertion, including a false assertion, contradiction or non-supporting citation in grounding, makes that dimension incorrect. Otherwise, any unverified required item or extra makes it unverified. Otherwise, every required item and extra must be correct (fully supported for grounding) for a correct assessment. Of the remaining cases, any correct/partial required item or extra makes the assessment partial; otherwise any tool error makes it error; otherwise all required items unsupported makes it unsupported; all other combinations of missing/unsupported items make it missing. Keep operational limitations and their affected IDs alongside this assessment; partial, missing, unsupported, unverified and error outcomes never become full passes. These are accounting rules, not owner quality thresholds.
Report planned and executed counts and separate dimension-level outcomes, retaining missing dependency/license/environment, protected/removed diagram and tool-error records. Preserve gaps by family, save version, native reading version and target/module; no unavailable case becomes a full-read pass.
Deliverable: reviewed development case specifications and a restricted case-schema equivalent, with provenance and coverage mapping. Missing now: native semantic observations, independently checked answers and dedicated cases for uncovered families/versions.

## oracle

Establish and freeze expected claims before exposing the case to the evaluated reader. The reader's export, reconstructed diagram or answer is never an answer key or the sole evidence for a claim.
Preserve and hash original bytes before conversion. Trusted preparation jobs operate on separately hashed working copies; record original save-version evidence separately from native LabVIEW version/build, OS, target, installed modules, licenses and dependency resolution. A successful load in one environment does not establish compatibility elsewhere.
Request bounded native cloud jobs with declared artifact hashes, permitted operations, required LabVIEW versions and staged output schemas. Roles receive results, not Docker daemon access. Existing NI 2026 Q1 readiness is environment evidence only; inventory inspection, VI Scripting, other versions and especially native 2024 need their own readiness checks.

| Claim type | Independent establishment procedure | Evidence required for the key |
|---|---|---|
| Structure, types and properties | Inspect preserved originals in native LabVIEW; use native properties/diagrams and a reviewer-readable capture/object map. Compare text declarations directly with original XML/configuration where applicable. | Original hash and resolvable locator, native property values/captures, inspection method and any inaccessible object. |
| Computation and execution dependencies | Choose controlled inputs and initial state; record outputs, errors and relevant execution observations. Inspect dataflow/structures to explain dependencies; one observed schedule cannot prove an ordering guarantee. | Input/output vectors, reset procedure, actual run logs, relevant diagrams and version-specific documentation for semantics. |
| Typedef and diagram changes | Preserve both original revisions; apply a documented edit to derived copies, inspect affected consumers/native differences and run discriminating controlled inputs. | Before/after hashes, edit recipe, affected/unaffected checks, native difference evidence and observed behavior. |
| Projects, packages and builds | Inspect native project/package properties and original declarations; explicitly check resolution and, where authorized, installation/build behavior in an isolated job. | Declared versus resolved dependency lists, member identities, build settings/results and unresolved items. |
| Targets, hardware and compiled artifacts | Inspect available native interfaces and run only with the required runtime, target and hardware or declared simulator. | Actual execution environment and observed scope; simulation or structural inspection cannot certify hardware behavior or recover removed diagrams. |

Documentation supports semantic interpretation only when pinned by version, section/location and content hash or preserved permitted excerpt; it does not establish what a particular original diagram contains. Unavailable documentation remains a gap.
An independent checker must resolve each expected claim and citation against original/native evidence without using the reader's output to derive the answer. Record case/key revision, evidence hashes, checker identity/role and session, procedure, claim-level agreement/disagreement and unresolved uncertainty. Disagreement blocks that claim's use as an established key until adjudicated and rechecked.
Use more than one observable when necessary to distinguish plausible wrong answers: selected inputs must separate intended computation from known alternatives, and native structural evidence must support dependencies not distinguishable by outputs alone. Record any underdetermined behavior explicitly.
Predeclare numeric precision, units, comparison rules, nondeterministic behavior and allowed variation per case. Missing runtime, dependencies, licenses, native diagram access or independent check yields an unavailable/unverified key and an unexecuted or limited case, never a passing semantic result.
Deliverable: independently checked claim/key records, native job recipes and substantive evidence, public only for development cases. Missing now: approved native inspection/experiment capability, version/module coverage, completed native jobs and independent checker records; this draft supplies no oracle answers.

## split

Treat all prepared public sources, their history and derivatives as development material. Public availability prevents a credible claim of withholding or model unfamiliarity; placing cases or keys under an obscure repository path, release asset or evaluator directory provides no isolation.
Before allocation, group whole projects with shared dependencies, copied components and related revisions into lineage groups. Detect exact copies by original/member hashes; investigate renamed files, resaved versions, archive duplicates and edited variants using provenance, native structural comparisons and manual review. Hash inequality alone does not establish independence. Record the method, candidate matches, adjudications and remaining uncertainty.
Allocate entire connected lineage groups to one partition; never split a project into development and evaluation files or permit a shared answer-bearing dependency to bridge partitions. Resolve ambiguous relationships conservatively before measurement. Deduplicate reporting while preserving an inventory-to-group map and visible coverage gaps.
Use two declared evaluation strata: unseen project lineages within represented families, and whole held-out families with all their related projects/components excluded from development. Assign mixed-family projects as complete groups; if this makes a family holdout infeasible, report the missing stratum rather than leaking components across it. Publish only nonrevealing aggregate split descriptions.
Commission new owner-authorized cases in the restricted environment, with fresh computations, dependencies and controlled changes designed independently of the reader. Keep originals, authoring history, native captures and keys restricted; record ownership/license and check for overlap with public/development material. New cases reduce public-example familiarity risk but cannot prove absence from every model's training data.
A trusted custodian maintains the restricted allocation manifest, hashes, lineage decisions, key revisions and access audit. Development sessions receive development inputs only. Evaluation runners release only the assigned question and permitted artifact views to a fresh evaluated session; keys and oracle evidence remain accessible only to the scorer/checker. Evaluation inputs necessarily revealed during a run must not return to development contexts.
Before any measurement, test the actual boundary with representative development and evaluated-session identities: deny access to withheld storage, keys, other cases, repository history containing restricted material, caches, mounts, credentials, logs and artifacts. Check allowed access works; use synthetic sentinel material to test denial and output/log leakage without exposing real keys. Record identities, operations, results and independent review. A declared ACL without executed checks is insufficient.
Pin split revision, case/key hashes and access configuration before runs. Disable cross-run memory and shared workspaces; audit provider retention and training settings against the concrete data grant. Restrict transcripts and detailed scores that reveal cases; a leak invalidates the affected holdout and requires replacement and renewed isolation checks before measurement resumes.
Deliverable: restricted lineage/allocation manifest and executed access-boundary evidence, with a redacted public summary. Missing now: a concrete restricted store/runner, custodian, private/new cases, variant analysis and tested isolation. Public sources can support development specifications and duplicate-method preparation now, not withheld evaluation.

## baseline

Compare paired runs of each eligible task without and with the reader. Both arms receive the same question, original artifact set, permitted documentation and native panel/diagram images where available; only reader access differs. Record unavailable images and unsupported modalities explicitly. Give text artifacts directly in both arms; do not silently equip the no-tool arm with another semantic exporter.
Pin a run manifest before comparison: provider and exact model snapshot, model/image capabilities, generation settings and seeds where supported, full common prompt and input hashes, the minimal arm-specific tool instruction, reader commit/configuration, tool schema, runner/scorer versions, split/key revision and native evidence versions. If the provider cannot pin a snapshot, disclose that limitation and bound the run window; do not claim exact reproducibility.
Use identical total token, context, output and wall-time budgets, including reader output in the tool arm's context budget. Declare tool-call limits and count their time/cost. Context truncation and budget exhaustion remain recorded outcomes. Keep case inputs, presentation order and scoring rules matched; randomize paired arm order to reduce time/provider effects.
Before seeing comparative results, freeze the number of independent repetitions per case/arm, sampling order, stopping rule and affordable run ceiling in the manifest. Choose these through a documented precision/cost plan using development pilots only; no numerical repetition count or spending grant is established by this draft. A missing count, exhausted quota or incomplete pair prevents a completed comparison and must be reported.
Run each repetition in a fresh session without prior answers, key access or shared memory. Preserve actual requests, responses, tool calls, images, model identifiers, timestamps, token usage, errors and scorer records in the appropriate restricted boundary. Reuse the same assigned case/key revision across the pair; do not tune prompts or select favorable repeats using evaluation results.
Pin the task reference inventories, claim-mapping/adjudication rules, dimension-level outcome rules and fixed denominators in the run manifest before either arm runs. Measure answer correctness and source grounding separately for both arms using the same required answer IDs; retain omissions, extras, duplicates and contradictions in each arm's scoring records.
Measure reader fidelity through the frozen reader-observation contract in tasks, independently of agent answers. Pin probe-suite and native-reference revisions in the manifest and execute every required probe in a separate recorded reader session under predeclared limits, isolated from the paired answer sessions. Preserve raw requests/responses and the complete alignment/outcome ledger; agent-selected calls provide supplementary evidence only. Report probe time/cost separately from paired answer-session time/cost. Do not invent a reader-fidelity result for a no-tool arm that produces no reader representation: mark it not applicable, without a fidelity pass or a paired fidelity-gain estimate.
Report paired case-level results and distributions by family, original save version, native reading version and target/module. Publish planned, attempted, executed, scored and complete-pair counts, including missing keys/runtime, unsupported cases and failures. Show required-item outcome counts over each frozen dimension denominator and the separate extra-claim ledger; disclose incomplete probe suites and unprobed scope. Do not drop unavailable cases from coverage or treat them as successful observations.
Record elapsed end-to-end and tool time, input/output tokens, provider-reported or explicitly estimated cost with pinned price basis, and infrastructure cost where applicable. Disclose cached requests, retries, queue time and unpriced resources rather than equating missing cost data with zero. Existing subscription limits do not authorize extra spending.
Predeclare per-case numeric tolerances and nondeterminism handling from the oracle evidence. Separately predeclare an uncertainty procedure for comparative estimates: use paired differences and resampling at independent project/lineage level, preserving repetitions within each group; record confidence level, resampling seed/count and treatment of missing pairs before evaluation. Report small-sample limitations and avoid treating related files or repeats as independent projects.
Do not select comparison tolerances, exclusions, aggregation weights or uncertainty settings after observing which arm wins. Show separate dimensions and coverage strata; any quality threshold, acceptable uncertainty target or claim that a gain satisfies the goal requires owner acceptance.
Deliverable: frozen run manifest, reproducible paired runner/scoring procedure, substantive run evidence and separate uncertainty/cost/time reports. Missing now: accepted cases/keys/split, tested isolation, concrete model/provider quota, repetition/precision plan and an independently reviewed executable scorer. No matched runs have been executed by this draft.

## challenges

Exercise the proposed scoring method on independently keyed development cases before evaluation. Freeze the expected challenge outcomes first; retain exact submitted answers/exports, original evidence, scorer output and an independent claim-level review. Valid filenames, hashes and successful process exit alone do not establish that the method detects wrong content.

| Deliberate challenge | Discriminating setup | Required observable result |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong answer | Start from a checked correct response; change a substantive computation, dependency or typedef-impact claim while retaining its valid source locator. Use controlled inputs/native evidence that distinguish the replacement claim. | Correctness detects the false claim; citation resolution alone cannot make it correct. Retain the true and false claims and evidence explaining the difference. |
| Wrong citation | Keep a correct answer but cite a different existing object, case branch, member or revision that does not support it; also test an unresolvable locator. | Grounding rejects the unsupported citation independently of answer correctness. Checking only that the file exists or has a valid hash must fail this challenge. |
| Selective omission | Remove one required answer claim and, separately, one required reader observation from otherwise checked complete submissions containing at least two required IDs in the affected dimension; retain the remaining correct content and citations. | The omitted ID remains missing in its frozen denominator; correctness/grounding or fidelity respectively becomes partial, never complete. True extra claims cannot replace the omitted ID. |
| Extra false claim | Add an independently falsified assertion outside the required inventory to an otherwise correct answer and, separately, to a reader response; include an existing but non-supporting citation for the answer assertion. | The extra ledger retains the false assertion; answer correctness and grounding, or fidelity respectively, becomes incorrect despite all required IDs matching. Required denominators remain unchanged. Also test a true extra and an unverified extra: neither adds required credit, and the unverified extra prevents a correct case assessment. |
| Duplicate and contradiction | Repeat a correct assertion, then separately add its negation while retaining the original, in answers and reader responses. | Duplicates earn credit once and are counted separately; contradictions retain both raw assertions and make the affected item and dimension incorrect. Repetition cannot increase the denominator or conceal a missing ID. |
| Constant export | Feed one fixed plausible export to the frozen probes for at least two cases with independently established incompatible facts, such as different types or computations. Preserve raw probe requests/responses and native-reference alignments. | Fidelity detects the contradicted/missing required observations on the discriminating case using the frozen denominator; plausible formatting and generic facts cannot earn full fidelity. Inspect both substantive comparisons independently of agent answers. |
| Empty, truncated or failed probe | Submit empty content and a syntactically valid wrapper containing no facts; separately truncate required content, return an unsupported response and cause a controlled reader error. | Raw requests, responses and failures remain recorded against every required probe/observation ID. Absent facts remain missing; unsupported/error conditions retain their explicit records and never pass. Neither process success, schema validity nor a correct agent answer supplies fidelity. |
| Unsupported or inaccessible case | Use a declared unsupported family and controlled missing-runtime/dependency/license or inaccessible-diagram conditions. | Preserve the exact limitation and planned/attempted/executed counts. Honest abstention may be recorded separately but never becomes full reading fidelity or demonstrated behavior. |
| Missing evidence or execution | Remove the independent key, omit the native run, or supply a report listing zero executed tests. | Results remain unverified/unexecuted; the scorer cannot award a semantic pass, infer a key from the reader, or count a planned test as executed. |

Include positive controls: independently checked correct claims and correct locators must receive their expected dimension-level assessments. Otherwise a scorer that rejects everything could appear to detect every deliberate error. Challenge expectations concern discriminating facts and evidence, not owner quality thresholds.
Inspect actual outputs for implicit claims: loading is not execution; structural inspection is not hardware behavior; export success is not semantic fidelity; a matching answer is not grounded unless its original location supports it. Record unchecked outcomes and any assertion that would also accept the deliberately wrong output.
A failed challenge blocks use of the affected scoring claim until the method is revised and independently rechecked. Keep failed outputs and explanations; do not relabel absent runtime/key as a passing challenge. This draft executes no challenges and supplies no challenge results.
Deliverable: repeatable development challenge fixtures, expected outcomes established independently, executed substantive comparisons and separate reviewer findings. Missing now: checked development keys/native evidence, an executable scoring method and independent review. Only the evaluator role, on Policymaker order and with its separate verifier, may change `policy/evaluator/`; benchmark preparation does not grant that authority.
