## Authority classes

`declared` (asserted in the bytes), `locally_computed` (hash/size/sequence recomputed; byte consistency only), `stipulated_synthetic` (a synthetic premise, never satisfies a real requirement), `independently_verified` (needs a receipt from a party independent of the producer naming a verifier in an external registry) and `unavailable`. The registry and receipts are null today, so nothing can be independently verified and every real requirement fails closed. An `independently_verified` claim whose verifier is not registered counts as a rejected claim and is downgraded to declared. A locally generated hash chain or an append-only claim never establishes provenance.

## Receipt, ledger and acyclic binding

The freeze receipt is an external document; none exists. JSON `/freeze_receipt/fields` gives each field with the authority it needs (policy/manifest/plan/settings hashes, ordered slot tuples, comparison maps, order derivation, preflight evidence, owner records, ceilings, reservation rule, ledger identity/storage, producer, freeze UTC, verification receipt reference). Binding order: reviewed policy → manifest → plan → settings → derived dispatch order → receipt → independent verification of receipt → ledger events. The receipt never contains its own hash or any event hash; each event cites the receipt hash and only earlier events.

Each event has `ledger_id`, `receipt_sha256`, `seq`, `segment`, `event_type`, `event_utc`, `prev_event_sha256`, `event_sha256`, `persistence`, producer with authority class, full slot identity, `attempt_id`, `attempt_order`, `delivery_id` (new, distinct from attempt identity), `evidence_refs` and typed payload. `seq` alone orders; UTC corroborates. Gaps, chain/hash mismatches, UTC regression or unacknowledged persistence end the **verified prefix**. Later events remain retained but unverified: they contribute nothing to inventories or completion, and the slots they touch become indeterminate (ineligible and unresolved), so a count can only fall.

## Events, gates and transitions

Events: `preflight_result`, `reservation_made`, `dispatch_recorded`, `delivery_retained`, `delivery_interpreted`, `usage_reconciled`, `slot_closed`, `stop_recorded`, `plan_terminal`, `final_audit`, `audit_revision`, `submission_quarantined`, `evidence_gap_recorded`, `recovery_opened`. Slot cycle: reservation → dispatch → delivery retained **before** interpretation → usage → close. Dispatch needs: no stop or terminal, no other open slot, slot next in the frozen order, a reservation for that slot, acknowledged write-ahead persistence and retained request bytes. A gate failure becomes a stop cause (`hard_evidence_failure` > `settings_drift` > `budget`), all causes kept in detection order. A stop on the last slot keeps the stop state. After terminal only retention events and audits are legal. JSON `/gate_inputs`, `/event_types` and `/plan_states` give the full tables, and `/policy_rule_crosswalk` maps R02–R16.

## Decision table and handoff

From the retained bytes alone: `ROW` (dispatched, complete evidence; a unique envelope row, `legacy_record` only if unchanged legacy validation accepts, else null), `ROW_UNRESOLVED` (representable distinct-ID extra), `DELIVERY_ONLY` (identical redelivery with proof; no row, no charge), `DELIVERY_ONLY_UNPROVED` (both kept, slot unresolved, hard stop), `QUARANTINE` (conflicting same-ID bytes, unrepresentable or unplanned submission; raw bytes in the rejection ledger, no row), `BUNDLE_NO_ROW` (dispatched attempt with missing/corrupt required evidence or no delivery; explicit gap, no row) and `NONE`. These terms exist only in ledger and audit outputs; no envelope enum, schema field, scoring denominator or caller boundary is added. A real `DELIVERY_ONLY` needs independently verified delivery-only proof that does not exist; the ledger cannot confer it and equal response text never suffices. A slot closes `attempted_eligible` only for a single dispatched attempt with `ROW`, complete success, valid legacy record, passing postdispatch checks and no extra, conflict or unproved delivery. Later evidence never edits a closure; it appears as `audit_revision`.

## Inventories, accounting, crash and audit

Consumers derive planned, authorized dispatches, dispatched, retained attempts, deliveries, missing, unstarted, indeterminate, ineligible, unresolved and structural comparisons from verified events (definitions in `/inventories`). Planned coverage and comparison denominators never shrink; nothing is imputed. Legacy multiple-attempt selection stays unresolved with zero completion. Reservations are whole-slot worst case; `usage_reconciled` is once per distinct attempt (extras included), proven redelivery adds no charge. Unknown usage keeps usage and cost null, cannot satisfy a required ceiling and forces a hard stop; unknown prices stay null.

Crash rules C1–C8: write-ahead dispatch; unverifiable dispatch persistence leaves the slot indeterminate since a send cannot be excluded; an acknowledged dispatch without delivery is `BUNDLE_NO_ROW` with no invented interrupted event and no declared absent response; a recovery segment may only record gaps, quarantines, a stop and `stopped_hard_failure`, never reserve, dispatch, resume or retry. `final_audit` (revision 0) fixes operational completion at terminal; later `audit_revision`s may lower it while the terminal state and earlier audits stay visible. Irrecoverable storage/evidence gaps are reported, not hidden.

## Unavailable authority (fail closed)

Receipt verification and verifier registry; storage-enforced append-only immutability; trusted clock; persistence acknowledgement authority; provider usage, price and returned model identity; delivery-only proof; isolation, routing and restricted storage; owner precision, quota and acceptance records. Each yields a blocked preflight, declared-only provenance, unknown usage or an unproved delivery as listed in JSON `/external_authority_required_fail_closed`. Real-envelope admission remains blocked. Actual admissions, model/reader/native executions, scored runs, semantic passes and real comparisons are **zero**; costs null.
