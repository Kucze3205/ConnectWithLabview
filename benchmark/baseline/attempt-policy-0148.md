## Attempt decisions and stopping

R05–R13 prohibit automatic retries, replacement and response reuse. A failed/interrupted/unsupported answer, absent/empty/binary-invalid/malformed response or legacy-invalid record remains retained and ineligible. With complete evidence and infrastructure, continue the finite order without retry. Missing/corrupt required evidence, identity collision, isolation/storage failure, unavailable usage needed for ceilings, external interruption or required-infrastructure failure triggers an immediate hard stop. An ordinary unsupported answer is different from unsupported required infrastructure. Preserve original evidence that was received; if preservation itself fails, record the missing/corrupt objects and irrecoverable gap rather than claim lossless retention succeeded or manufacture replacements. Unknown attempt outcomes are quarantine diagnostics, not new envelope enum values.

A byte-identical redelivery of the **same attempt** creates another immutable delivery record, not a second envelope attempt. This requires full-submission identity and trusted delivery-only proof that there was no second provider execution; equal response text is insufficient. Ambiguous proof or conflicting same-ID content is an unresolved hard evidence failure: retain all deliveries and quarantine conflicting submissions. A distinct attempt ID is an unsolicited extra even if its response bytes are identical. Retain it, reject it under this policy, leave envelope selection unresolved and give its comparison zero completion. During dispatch this stops the plan before the next slot. A late extra updates the final audit/completion inventory without reopening dispatch or erasing the recorded operational terminal history. Any future retry-enabled variant needs its own predeclared reviewed policy and freeze; it cannot reinterpret these attempts retrospectively.

Predispatch settings/input/manifest/plan/model-window drift stops without creating an attempt. Postdispatch drift retains the attempt as ineligible and stops. Restoring settings does not resume this freeze. Before each request reserve the entire slot worst-case allocation within frozen matched per-request limits, total quota/token/time/call ceilings and run window. Missing initial affordability blocks preflight; later depletion or an exhausted request budget stops dispatch. Retain usage, errors, truncation, cache/retry/queue/tool time, separate probe resources and all known charges, including unsolicited attempts. Unknown prices/costs remain null; no overrun, new quota or spending is authorized. Unreconcilable resource evidence is a hard evidence failure.

Plan states are `ready`, `running`, then terminal `blocked_preflight`, `finished_complete`, `finished_incomplete`, `stopped_hard_failure`, `stopped_settings_drift` or `stopped_budget`. First detected stop prevents further dispatch; record every cause, with same-gate primary precedence evidence failure, settings drift, budget. A stop on the last slot still records a stopped state. Exhausting the order without a hard stop is `finished_complete` only if every planned comparison structurally completes and audit is clear; otherwise `finished_incomplete`. Terminal history never authorizes restart. Final audit and completion counts are separate and may worsen with late evidence.

Slot outcomes distinguish unstarted with cause, attempted eligible, attempted ineligible and unresolved (always ineligible for completion). Keep planned, dispatched, retained-attempt/delivery, missing, unstarted, ineligible and unresolved inventories separately. Missing means no retained attempt references that planned slot; unstarted means no authorized dispatch, so an unsolicited attempt can make those inventories differ. Preserve rejected/unplanned originals outside a valid envelope when needed. Never impute an arm or shrink planned coverage.

Exactly one byte-bound legacy-valid complete successful attempt in each explicitly mapped opposite-arm slot is necessary for **synthetic structural completion**; errors, truncation, interruption, exhaustion, failed tools, missing evidence and multiple attempts prevent it. This confers no semantic credit. Independently scored real completion needs the governing evidence/scoring method; actual real comparisons here remain zero. Correctness and grounding retain separate D1–D4 denominators of 4, with extras/omissions/duplicates/contradictions preserved; fidelity remains a separate required unavailable probe suite, no-tool not applicable.
